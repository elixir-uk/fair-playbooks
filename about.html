About

About this playbook

The FAIR-in-action Playbook gives the people who support researchers ready-made FAIR activities they can pick, adapt to their institution and run. It is built by the ELIXIR-UK RDM Club and commissioned by BioFAIR as part of the Pathfinder project The FAIR-in-action Bridge.

Accessibility statement

We want everyone who runs or takes part in a session to be able to use this site. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What you can do on this site

  • Use it with a keyboard only. The first Tab stop is a "Skip to content" link, and every link and button shows a clear focus outline.
  • Read all text at a contrast of at least 4.5:1, including when an institution's own colours are applied. The kit darkens or lightens a colour automatically where it would otherwise be too faint.
  • Zoom the page to 200% without losing content.
  • Turn on "reduce motion" in your device settings to stop the animations (the 404 static, the falling pile of data, the slide transitions and the running character).
  • Read text without italics: we don't use them, because they are harder to read for many people, including dyslexic readers.
  • Present the slides with a keyboard: press ? on a slide for all shortcuts.

Known limitations

  • Pixel font. Short labels, numbers and menu items use a pixel-style font. We keep it to a few words, at about 11 pixels or larger; body text is always in Lato.
  • Posters. PNG posters from the promo kit are images. When you share one, also share the announcement text (the "Copy announcement text" button) so people using screen readers get the details.
  • Downloads. The PowerPoint and Word files are generated automatically. They use real headings and lists, but we haven't tested them with screen readers yet.
  • Screen readers. We have tested with automated checks and keyboard only. We haven't yet tested with screen-reader users.

How we tested

Last tested on 15 November 2026: automated checks of colour contrast, labels, link and button names, image text alternatives and heading order on every type of page, in the default colours and two institution colour schemes; keyboard testing; and reduced-motion testing. There has been no independent audit.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site doesn't work for you, please open an issue on GitHub and describe the page and what went wrong. We will reply and fix what we can.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. We don't collect, receive or store any of your information.

What stays in your browser

  • Your institution profile (name, contacts, colours and logo) is saved in your browser's local storage when you press Save on the set-up page. It is never sent to us. Remove it with "Remove" in the banner or by clearing your browser data.
  • Poll counts on the live slides are saved in your browser so a page refresh doesn't lose them. Press Reset to clear them.
  • Files and names you add (a QR code on the promo page, participant names on the certificates page) are used on that page only. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

Services that see your visit

Like any website, loading a page shares your IP address with the services that deliver it:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts. See Google Fonts privacy.
  • jsDelivr provides the slide software (reveal.js) on the live slides pages. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.
