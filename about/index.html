The FAIR-in-action Playbook gives the people who support researchers ready-made FAIR activities they can pick, adapt to their institution and run. It is built by the ELIXIR-UK RDM Club and commissioned by BioFAIR as part of the Pathfinder project The FAIR-in-action Bridge.

Who it's for

The playbook is for anyone who helps researchers manage and share their data: RDM professionals, data stewards, librarians, trainers and research software engineers.

It was made for the life sciences, so the examples, datasets and resources come from biology and health research. The activities themselves work in any field. To use them elsewhere, swap the examples for ones from your own discipline, and point people to the repositories and standards they use.

What's in it

  • Modules: sessions with a facilitator script, live slides, a PowerPoint and a facilitator guide.
  • Games: short, hands-on activities that win people over before they care about FAIR. Run one as a drop-in stand in a faculty foyer or atrium, as a hook at a lab meeting, or as a warm-up inside a module.
  • Journeys: ready-made paths through several modules.
  • Promo kit and certificates: posters to advertise a session and certificates of attendance.

Each activity is built on well-established resources such as RDMkit, FAIR Cookbook and Galaxy, and each module lists what it is built from.
