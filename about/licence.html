Reusing the playbook

The playbook's own content is licensed CC BY 4.0: you can reuse and adapt it, for any purpose, as long as you credit it.

Suggested credit: "FAIR-in-action Playbook, ELIXIR-UK RDM Club, commissioned by BioFAIR. CC BY 4.0."

Material from others

Material we reuse from others keeps its own licence. It is credited where it appears: in each module's "Built from" list, and on the closing slide of every deck and in every facilitator guide.

Artwork, fonts and software

  • Pixel art. The characters and icons are original artwork for this playbook, under the same CC BY 4.0 licence.
  • Fonts. Lato and Press Start 2P, both under the SIL Open Font License.
  • Software. The live slides use reveal.js (MIT). The PowerPoint and Word downloads are made in your browser with pptxgenjs and docx (both MIT).
