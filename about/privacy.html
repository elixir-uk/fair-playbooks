This site has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. We don't collect, receive or store any of your information.

What stays in your browser

  • Your institution profile (name, contacts, colours and logo) is saved in your browser's local storage when you press Save on the set-up page. It is never sent to us. Remove it with "Remove" in the banner or by clearing your browser data.
  • Poll counts on the live slides are saved in your browser so a page refresh doesn't lose them. Press Reset to clear them.
  • Files and names you add (a QR code on the promo page, participant names on the certificates page) are used on that page only. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

Services that see your visit

Like any website, loading a page shares your IP address with the services that deliver it:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts. See Google Fonts privacy.
  • jsDelivr provides the slide software (reveal.js) on the live slides pages. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.
