Games

Short, hands-on games that get people talking about their data before they care about FAIR, or have even heard of it. Set one up as a drop-in stand in a faculty foyer or atrium and catch people as they walk past, use it as the hook at a lab meeting, induction or open day, or run it as a warm-up at the start of a module. Each comes with a facilitator run-sheet and printable handouts, and published games are credited to their authors.
