Games

Short, hands-on games that get people talking about their data before they care about FAIR, or have even heard of it. Use one as the hook at a lab meeting, an induction or an open day, or as a warm-up at the start of a module. Each comes with a facilitator run-sheet and printable handouts, and published games are credited to their authors.
