Set up your institution

Fill this in once. Your repositories, contacts and policies flow into every activity, slide placeholder and "local help" box across the kit – on this site. Nothing is forked, so when the RDM Club ships a new activity, you get it instantly, already wired to these details.

Why not just fork the repo? Because a fork freezes the day you make it – every new module and fix upstream would pass you by. This profile lives in your browser and rides on top of the always-current kit.

Share one profile across your team

If a colleague has already set this up, import their profile file and you're done. Once you've saved your own, export it and send the file to your team.
